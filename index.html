I really want to make a rhythm game at some point, but for now I'm just spitting out ideas. Here's what I have so far: For starters, it's inspired by grind fiction, the 90s-2000s era, and pop culture monsters. You play as a band/party of up to 5 characters, each with different playstyles. Basically, you'd run around levels, find clusters of enemies, and beat them up for money (because you're just street performers annoying the public). I want it to be at least rhythm beat-em-up/dungeon crawler, but that might be a tad ambitious. But yeah, that's the basic rundown.
